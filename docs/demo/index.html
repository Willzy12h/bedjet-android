Interactive demo

Nocturne

Plans each night on a BedJet 3 around your alarm. Tap around it as you would on a phone.

This is an HTML version of the app, not the app itself. It shows the main features with a pretend BedJet, so it isn't one-for-one and nothing connects to a real device. Newer builds of the app may look or work differently.

Things to try

    Use Demo time at the bottom of the phone to play or scrub through the night. The browser's back and forward buttons work too.
