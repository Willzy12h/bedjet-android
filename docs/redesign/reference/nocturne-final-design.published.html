Nocturne · app design · final

The final design, ready to map to the code

Round 4 with the palette and tile decisions. Preset tiles, steps and profile cards now share one style: a coloured stripe down the left side and a flat, soft tint of their colour, with no fades. There are seven palettes: six for night, and Overcast by day with a sky-blue Start button. Below is every screen we're building, in order.

Colour

Ember red, deeper as it gets hotter

Locked in. Warm 26–30° is red-orange, 32° is a clear red, 36° and up is deeper, and Turbo at 43° is the deepest. Cool stays blue in every palette.

Top: a summer night, cool all night, a warm wake-up, then the 10-minute cooldown. Bottom: a winter night, Turbo, hot start, red easing to red-orange, a wake-up and the cooldown.

Profile editor

P2, with the after-waking cooldown and the step editor

Steps can now be any length, from 1 minute to 12 hours, except Turbo, which the BedJet fixes at 10 minutes. The night still ends at your alarm. Every new profile now has one step after it: Cool at fan 30% for 10 minutes, then off. It is an ordinary step, so you can change it, drag it or delete it. Adding a step opens the step editor straight away, so you set the time, temperature and fan there.

Through the night

R1, with a fan shape

The temperature works as before: the night is split into equal blocks from From to To, set with − and + or by dragging the first or last block. New: the fan has its own shape, chosen separately, so any temperature shape can have any fan shape. Steady keeps one fan speed. Ease down moves from one speed to another with the temperature blocks. Quiet middle runs a settling speed at first, a quiet speed while you sleep, and a stronger speed before your wake-up. Drag the dots up or down to set each speed, and drag the edges to set when it changes.

By day · Overcast

The main screens in the day palette

The same screens as above, in Overcast. This checks that the cards, stripes, charts and sheets all hold up on a light page.

Palettes

Seven palettes: six for night, one for day

Midnight Ink is the night default and Overcast is the day default. Overcast is a soft blue-grey page with lighter cards and firm edges, no white, and a sky-blue Start button. The night extras are Forest, Graphite OLED for true black, Plum, Autumn · Rust and Winter · Storm. Blue is always cool and heat is always Ember red. Each palette is shown on home and on a home-screen widget.

Locked

Everything decided

colourEmber redOne function maps temperature to colour for bars, tiles, steps, charts and widgets. Cool stays blue.
shapeSquarer bar endsOuter ends match the Start button's corners. Inner joins stay square.
shapeSide stripePreset tiles, steps and profile cards have a 4px stripe down the left side in their mode or main colour, over a flat, soft tint of that colour. No gradients.
stepsAny length1 minute to 12 hours per step. Turbo fixed at 10 minutes. Heat limits from the BedJet are handled by joining steps back to back.
rampFan shapesSteady, Ease down or Quiet middle, independent of the temperature shape.
cooldownAfter wakingNew profiles get Cool, fan 30%, 10 minutes, then off. Existing profiles are offered it once.
palettesSeven palettesMidnight Ink by night and Overcast by day as defaults. Forest, Graphite OLED, Plum, Autumn · Rust and Winter · Storm as extras. Overcast uses a sky-blue Start button (#2f93dc) and a deeper blue for cool (#2b8ee0). Widgets follow the palette.
linksGitHubThe repo is public. Help and about links to the project page for now; where releases live is decided later.
finishBefore buildEmpty and error states, Undo, an accessibility pass, History mapped to what the app records, and every sheet checked in the light palettes.
