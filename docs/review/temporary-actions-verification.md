## Chosen behaviour

The action dialogue offers **Temporarily, then resume** and **End tonight** for eligible Tonight Quick actions, Remote modes/Boost and the corresponding widget/shortcut actions. It captures the selected BedJet, exact night/revision and actual preset. Temporary is the initial choice when eligible; dismissal sends nothing. Changing its one-action duration does not change saved presets. Quick/widget/shortcut **End tonight** uses the original captured preset and original duration, even if the temporary slider or settings change while the dialogue waits. Ordinary Remote mode **End tonight** keeps its existing mode-only behaviour and adds no new timer; it must not display the temporary ten-minute duration as an ending promise.

The temporary choice uploads one complete BedJet programme: the temporary interval followed by the remaining immutable night schedule. It keeps the same night record ID, with a new revision and therefore a new `NightIdentity`. Wake stays at its agreed time; elapsed phases and already-started original Turbo are skipped, with Pause gaps where needed. A future original Turbo beginning exactly at return is retained. Another temporary action replaces the temporary interval using the retained underlying schedule. There is no phone alarm that later sends START, so returning does not require the app to reconnect. A phone clock reaching the planned return creates neither a command nor a newly confirmed History event.

Implementation defaults: existing Quick preset durations; Turbo at 10 minutes; Boost at its configured 10–30 minutes, with at most 10 minutes of Turbo followed by Heat; Remote modes at 10 minutes using the captured current target temperature/fan, clamped to the selected mode and saved profile limits. Temporary Turbo alone is limited to 10 minutes in its dialogue; other temporary modes allow a one-action duration up to 120 minutes. An action that cannot fit safely before wake-up, within the complete 32-step programme, is refused before writing. End tonight remains available.

Off keeps its existing ending action and tracked-night confirmation; it never offers temporary return. Wake Snooze/Extend actions retain their manual meaning at/after wake; sliders, Run for, Lights and Beeps keep their existing meanings. Stop tracking is local: it does not stop the programme on the unit. A physical Off or another programme can replace the continuation; a physical fan/temperature adjustment may leave the programme running. The app does not promise that every physical adjustment cancels a return.

An accepted changed alarm replans the base while preserving only the unelapsed temporary interval. A later alarm must not extend or restart that interval; an earlier alarm may shorten/remove it to preserve wake-up, with explanatory outcome/History wording. Unchanged observations remain quiet and local. Pending alarm observations survive the new revision.

These behaviour contracts are covered by the host evidence below; physical behaviour remains unverified. The core writes the full intended state inside the guarded transaction, including the first PENDING publication. Before a possible write it restores the matching prior state; after a possible write it durably retains UNKNOWN, with request-specific cleanup bounded to eight seconds. An already committed CONFIRMED state remains authoritative. The owner journals a pending alarm handoff before the revision can be confirmed, so a restart cannot strand the observation. That restart boundary and quiet-notification follow-up are recorded separately below.

Remote **End tonight** after a captured running night requires a matching selected mode **and programme step 0** before confirming that tracking ended. A matching mode while a programme is still running is UNKNOWN. Out-of-profile-limit actions must show a refusal rather than throw during a tap, and UNKNOWN tracking must not retain an old promised-return line.
