For **KL-01**, preserve external backups and inventory all three slots before writing. A fresh read of today's slot cannot recover its undocumented v1 original. For **KL-02**, the 15-minute period is a scheduler request, not a deadline or an immediate response to connecting power. Android documents that the application does not choose when a periodic job runs within its interval and that charging is a job constraint. Plugging in does not create a general exemption from background foreground-service restrictions. These platform constraints support the design's limits; the actual Samsung delay remains unmeasured. [Android JobInfo.Builder][P1]; [Android background-start restrictions][P2].

For **KL-04**, this is **not a rolling limit of 30 days before today**. Data older than 30 days today can still fall within the first-grant boundary. Android documents a new boundary after uninstall, reinstall and a new permission grant. Provider availability, record retention and permission state may impose further limits; an empty or failed overlay is not evidence that the person was awake. The reader handles missing permissions and read errors without inventing stages: [app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/platform/SleepOverlay.kt:13–25](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/platform/SleepOverlay.kt#L13-L25). ### KL-13: what remains visible after a lost notice

The runtime reason survives a completed save and is exposed through `NightStatus`, but Tonight renders that reason only for an unconfirmed or manually tracked night. It is therefore accurate to say that an UNKNOWN/manual night can still show the saved reason in the app. It is **not** accurate to promise that every terminal pre-write refusal remains visibly explained when the original night stays CONFIRMED: [app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/night/NightActions.kt:38–42](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/night/NightActions.kt#L38-L42); [app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/ui/TonightScreen.kt:124–133](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/ui/TonightScreen.kt#L124-L133).

Some apply failures also save an ERROR event, which the expanded History card displays. That supplies another in-app explanation **only when that event was written**; early preparation and original-backup refusals do not all take that path. A notice log is started asynchronously after notification handling begins, so it cannot guarantee recovery from death before that point: [app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/night/NightActions.kt:200–205](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/night/NightActions.kt#L200-L205), [263–273](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/night/NightActions.kt#L263-L273), [576–579](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/night/NightActions.kt#L576-L579); [app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/ui/HistoryScreen.kt:77–79](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/ui/HistoryScreen.kt#L77-L79); [app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/notify/Notifications.kt:154–161](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/notify/Notifications.kt#L154-L161), [205–209](../../app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/willzy12h/nocturne/notify/Notifications.kt#L205-L209).

**Implementation-owner decision remains open:** whether to expose every saved terminal reason while the old night stays CONFIRMED, or add durable notice delivery. This documentation does not resolve that behaviour. Physical crash-window capture and the actual recovery presentation remain **NOT RUN**; an uncaptured race is not a pass.
