## Items reserved for post-gate self-review

These are source-review concerns, not newly executed failures or permission to expand the feature set. The completed source/test classifications above must not hide them.

1. **Queued source-policy change and warning time.** `NightActions.kt:864` captures the initially prepared wake in `PendingRetime.requestedWakeAt`; `updateSettings` (`:1499`) does not invalidate it. Retry prepares with current settings (`:928`), and `core/.../night/NightRunner.kt:82` can choose a different profile fallback after unknown-source acceptance changes. The execution/warning still prefers the old stored time (`NightActions.kt:918,1080`). A focused regression should queue an accepted unknown alarm, disable acceptance, retry with a distinct fallback, force an uncertain write and compare the notice time with the actual prepared request. This concern is not yet reproduced by an executed regression.
2. **Background admission's combined lifetime.** `BedJetService.kt:214–221` creates a 115-second undelivered-submission deadline. `onStartCommand` (`:35–36`) removes the entry and cancels that deadline before owner admission. A delivery near the deadline can therefore continue the same receiver through a separately bounded owner wait (`NightActions.kt:151–160`). The UI `runOwned` path retains its outer bound; an absolute 115-second submission-to-admission lifetime for fire-and-forget background starts is not established. Review/fix this with a delayed-delivery regression rather than describing the separate bounds as one proved total.

R4 and R5 are explicit missing product behaviors, not newly implemented features. Native picker readability, force-stop widget delivery, reboot/unlock recovery, physical receipts, Samsung idle delivery and TalkBack remain unverified device acceptance. The widget's lack of an independent scheduled-theme wakeup is also an explicit source limitation; a new refresh scheduler was not added during this review.
