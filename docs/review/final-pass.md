## IR-02: current transport is not bound to the selected target

Evidence: `ble/GattLink.kt:70` sets the shared `wanted` flag before acquiring the connection mutex; `:72` accepts any ready session; `:74` captures the target address once; `:80` retries that address after backoff. Needed regression: fail A once, switch to B during retry backoff with a concurrent reconnect, assert no further A dial and no B command/read on A. ## IR-04: pre-start reports can still confirm a start
