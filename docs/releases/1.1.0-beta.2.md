## Nocturne 1.1.0-beta.2

The first public build of Nocturne, a free, open-source Android app for the BedJet 3 that plans each night around
your alarm. This is a beta: it's in daily use, but expect rough edges and please report anything odd as an issue.

**Download:** `nocturne-1.1.0-beta.2.apk` below. Android 12 or newer and a BedJet 3 are required. See the
[README](https://github.com/Willzy12h/bedjet-android#download) for install steps and what each permission is for.

### Highlights
- Night profiles that stretch to fit between bedtime and your alarm, and follow the alarm if you move it.
- Tonight's plan runs on the BedJet itself, so your phone can sleep.
- Remote, Boost, wake-up notification buttons, home-screen widgets and shortcuts.
- Auto-start, sleep history, optional Health Connect, and backups.
- No account, no cloud, no internet permission. [Privacy policy](https://github.com/Willzy12h/bedjet-android/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

### New in this beta
- Calendar and reminder alarms no longer move your wake-up; a night can start before you choose a wake time.
- The Remote's temperature bar stays still in Dry while the BedJet's lowest allowed temperature moves.
- Cool quick action runs for the BedJet's longest time (12 h) until you change it.
- A run time longer than a mode allows becomes the longest allowed, with a note, instead of being refused.
- The quick-action editor takes hours and minutes; the Remote has a Max run button and shows when a run ends.
- Tapping a preset on the grey (not connected) widget now connects to the BedJet and then runs it.
- Exact timing uses the "Alarms and reminders" permission, which you grant from Tonight's setup list.
