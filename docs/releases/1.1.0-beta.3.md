## Nocturne 1.1.0-beta.3

A beta of Nocturne, a free, open-source Android app for the BedJet 3 that plans each night around your alarm. Please
report anything odd as an issue.

**Download:** `nocturne-1.1.0-beta.3.apk` below. Android 12 or newer and a BedJet 3 are required. See the
[README](https://github.com/Willzy12h/bedjet-android#download) for install steps and what each permission is for.

### New in this beta
- Choose which apps' alarms count: Settings lists your clock apps and other apps that set alarms (white-noise and
  sleep apps, for example) with their real names and icons, and you tick any combination.
- When Tonight ignores an alarm from an app you haven't ticked, it names the app and lets you choose it. Calendar
  reminders are still ignored without comment.
- An alarm less than 3 hours after bedtime no longer turns into a very short night.
- About shows the installed version and has a **Check for a newer build** button that opens the releases in your
  browser. Nocturne still has no internet permission.
