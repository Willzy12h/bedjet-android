## Nocturne 1.1.0-beta.10

Open `nocturne-1.1.0-beta.10.apk` on your phone and choose **Update**. Keep your current Nocturne beta installed so Android carries your profiles, presets, selected BedJet and history across.

- Air and room readings hold their position as values change. Tonight and Remote share mode icons and a live status that distinguishes fresh reports from the last known state after a disconnect.
- Quick nap uses a moon icon and a soft neutral fill. Use **Nap options → Nap for…** to change the duration for one run while keeping your saved preset. The alarm row shows the wake source and opens its details.
- The profile editor highlights **After waking** and shows when a profile with no further steps switches off.
- Sleep distinguishes usable tracker sleep from BedJet activity. The recap identifies an observed standby or the last time the BedJet was seen running.

Nocturne is an independent app for Android 12 or newer and the BedJet 3. It installs alongside the manufacturer's BedJet app.

The APK digest is in `SHA256SUMS.txt` with the release assets. The public Nocturne signing certificate has SHA-256 `fd5fa67050e21e2f7e4c6926623ba75ac8236c0f53b72d057fa15cba34f40d81`.
