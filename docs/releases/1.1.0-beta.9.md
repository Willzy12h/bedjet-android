## Nocturne 1.1.0-beta.9

Open `nocturne-1.1.0-beta.9.apk` on your phone and choose **Update**. Keep your current public Nocturne beta installed so Android carries your profiles, presets, selected BedJet and history across.

Nocturne is an independent app for Android 12 or newer and the BedJet 3. It installs alongside the manufacturer's BedJet app.

- **Quick nap** starts for 90 minutes by default, followed by ten minutes of stronger cooling before switching off. Edit the duration and stages in **Nap options**, or choose a wake time or the next eligible alarm. Use your phone's Clock app for an audible alarm.
- **Tonight and Remote** show larger air and room temperatures. A small heating or cooling icon moves while fresh BedJet reports arrive and respects reduced motion. Profile previews show each step's duration, fan speed and mode.
- The untouched **Heat** preset now starts at 40°C for 30 minutes. **Long heat** starts at two hours and 30 minutes. Customised presets carry across the update.
- **Sleep** shows available tracker history for seven days, 30 days and three months. BedJet use and naps appear without a tracker. A stop time is shown as observed only when the BedJet reported standby.

Please test Quick nap's airflow transition and standby on a physical BedJet 3. Live Health Connect and physical BedJet verification are outstanding for this beta.
