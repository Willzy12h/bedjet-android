## Nocturne 1.1.0-beta.7

A beta of Nocturne, a free, open-source Android app for the BedJet 3 that plans each night around your alarm. Please
report anything odd as an issue.

**Download:** `nocturne-1.1.0-beta.7.apk` below. Android 12 or newer and a BedJet 3 are required. See the
[README](https://github.com/Willzy12h/bedjet-android#download) for install steps and what each permission is for.

This beta includes everything in [1.1.0-beta.6](https://github.com/Willzy12h/bedjet-android/releases/tag/v1.1.0-beta.6),
so you can install it straight over any earlier beta. Your profiles, fan speeds, temperatures and settings stay as you
left them.

This beta is about phones that hide alarms or close apps overnight.

### New in this beta
- **Can't see your alarm?** When Nocturne finds no alarm, Tonight shows "Alarm in another app?". It explains why some
  alarms, such as those set by white-noise apps, can't be seen, and offers tonight's wake time, a fixed wake time in
  the profile, or a choice of alarm apps.
- **Alarms from unnamed apps.** Android sometimes reports an alarm without saying which app set it. A new setting in
  Alarm apps chooses what happens: Ignore them, Ask each night (the default), or Always use them.
  - Ask each night: Tonight and the bedtime reminder offer the alarm, and nothing changes until you tap it. One tap
    uses it for tonight only.
  - Always use them asks first, since a calendar reminder could then change your wake time or when Auto-start begins
    a night.
  - **If you're upgrading:** earlier betas ignored these alarms. They're now offered each night instead; choose Ignore
    them to keep the old behaviour.
- **Battery tips for your phone.** On Honor, Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO, OnePlus, realme and vivo phones, the setup list
  shows the extra steps that stop the phone closing Nocturne overnight. "Open settings" goes straight to the right
  screen where it can.
- Before Android asks to let Nocturne run in the background, Nocturne explains why it needs this and what your phone's
  own battery controls are.
- The diagnostics export now includes your phone's maker, its battery settings for Nocturne, and why Android last
  closed the app, to help with reports from phones we can't test on.
- When a phone sends the same start-up, clock or alarm change several times in a row, Nocturne handles them together
  rather than one after another.
