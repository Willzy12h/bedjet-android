## Nocturne 1.3.0-beta.1

Open `nocturne-1.3.0-beta.1.apk` on your phone and choose **Update**. Keep your current Nocturne installed to retain your profiles, presets, BedJet pairing and history.

- **Tonight** shows the running programme and makes it easier to tell what the BedJet has confirmed. If the phone alarm moves, compare the new wake time with the one still programmed on the unit.
- **Profiles → Weekly schedule** shows the profile for each night and the default for unassigned nights. Choose a weekday to change its assignment or open a profile directly in the editor.
- **Tonight → Change → Use tonight** applies a profile for one night. The card shows when that choice expires, and **Follow schedule** returns to the usual profile.

Nocturne is an independent app for Android 12 or newer and the BedJet 3. It installs alongside the manufacturer's BedJet app.

The APK digest is in `SHA256SUMS.txt` with the release assets. The public Nocturne signing certificate has SHA-256 `fd5fa67050e21e2f7e4c6926623ba75ac8236c0f53b72d057fa15cba34f40d81`.
