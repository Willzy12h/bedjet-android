## Nocturne 1.1.0-beta.4

A beta of Nocturne, a free, open-source Android app for the BedJet 3 that plans each night around your alarm. Please
report anything odd as an issue.

**Download:** `nocturne-1.1.0-beta.4.apk` below. Android 12 or newer and a BedJet 3 are required. See the
[README](https://github.com/Willzy12h/bedjet-android#download) for install steps and what each permission is for.

### New in this beta
- Nocturne no longer writes to Health Connect. It only reads your sleep stages to show them beside your BedJet nights,
  and only if you turn that on. If you had turned on recording BedJet nights in an earlier beta, that is switched off
  when you update; anything already written stays in Health Connect until you delete it there.
- Times follow your phone's 12- or 24-hour setting everywhere: screens, notifications and widgets. Widget day names
  follow your phone's language.
- One setup list on Tonight, using the same names as Settings › Permissions, with a line on what each item is for.
  Samsung battery tips only appear on Samsung phones.
- Tonight explains the wake-up time once, in the label beside the time and a line you can tap for more.
- Moving your alarm during the night follows the same rules as at bedtime: alarms from apps you haven't ticked, and
  alarms too soon after bedtime, are ignored there too.
- The profile editor starts with the name and season.
- Clearer, consistent wording throughout, with no text smaller than 12 sp.
- If Nocturne crashes, the last crash is kept on the phone and included when you save a diagnostics file from
  Settings › About. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Screens stop listening for updates while they are in the background.
